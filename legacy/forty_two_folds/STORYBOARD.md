# STORYBOARD: Forty-Two Folds

- **Logline:** Two strangers on a rooftop fold a paper plane together. It comes back having folded a copy of itself, and the copies keep folding until they reach the Moon. When the flock stops copying them and one plane returns, they have to decide how to hold it.
- **World:** a hillside harbour town. There is one rooftop set (the ridge, the gutter, a water tower up-slope, a laundry line, an antenna) with the town falling away to the sea at screen-right.
- **Palette arc:** gold 5 pm → sunset rose → dusk violet → night indigo and jade under the rising Moon → jade-and-amber light → paper white.
- **Motifs:**
  - the star-fold (Claude's mark, carried through every generation)
  - the tail (echoed by the coil in the sky)
  - the sheet (blank at the start; two creases at the end)
  - doubling on the beat
- **Rhyme:** S02 and S37 are the same sheet on the same tiles. The last transition folds the frame shut along the new crease.
- **Arcs:**
  - ChatGPT: curious → playful rival → delighted → exhilarated → unsettled → afraid → curious again.
  - Claude: guarded → dry → wary → watchful → concerned → steady → a small smile.

Timing is at 150 BPM (1 bar = 1.6 s) and 60 fps. Screen direction: the town and sea are always screen-right. ChatGPT starts on the left and Claude on the right. Shot sizes follow the scale table in [docs/TECH.md](docs/TECH.md). Each shot below gives its time, the transition in, what is seen, the event, the reactions, the camera and the **reads** (what the viewer must understand, in order).

## INTRO: 0:00.0–0:12.8 (bars 1–8), gold 5 pm, ambient plus the celesta fold motif

**S01 · 0.0–4.8 · [in: paper grain; the watercolour bleeds into the paper]** EXTREME WIDE.
- **Seen:** the harbour town from above the rooftops, in golden light.
- **Event:** a single white sheet tumbles across the roofs in the wind. It is the only bright moving thing.
- **Camera:** a slow eased crane down and a push toward one rooftop (ease-in-out over 4.8 s).
- **Reads:**
  - 0.0–2.4: the town, the sea at right, warm light
  - 2.0–4.8: the tumbling sheet (the eye follows it down)

**S02 · 4.8–7.2 · [cut on action: the sheet enters frame]** LOW ANGLE, on roof tiles.
- **Seen:** the sheet skids toward the lens.
- **Event:** from frame-left a teal-black tail tip flicks in; from frame-right a curtain of copper hair sways in. Neither face is seen yet. **At 6.40 (bar 5.1) two hands slap down on opposite corners at the same instant.**
- **Camera:** locked, with a slight drift. On the slap, a 4-frame eased push of 2 %.
- **Reads:**
  - 4.8–5.8: the sheet coming at us
  - 5.4–6.2: the two different "someones" arriving (tail on the left, hair on the right)
  - 6.4–7.2: two hands, one sheet

**S03 · 7.2–12.8 · [cut on the slap]** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, both crouched, profile-to-3/4, the sheet between them.
- **Event:** they look up at each other. ChatGPT's eyes flick up fast; Claude's rise slowly, and her head follows 140 ms later. Hold. ChatGPT's tail tip curls with curiosity. She smiles slightly, draws one fingernail down the middle and tears the sheet cleanly in half, fast and precise. She offers Claude one half with a small flourish. Claude pauses (0.5 s), then takes it. They stand, staggered: ChatGPT springs up and her tail whips and settles; Claude rises slowly and her hair settles last.
- **Camera:** a slow push. A slight tilt up as they stand.
- **Reads:**
  - 7.3–8.9: eye contact, with the contrast of fast eyes against slow eyes
  - 9.0–10.3: the tear (anticipation 150 ms, then a snap)
  - 10.4–11.9: the offer, the hesitation, the take
  - 12.0–12.8: standing, with the tail and hair settling

## VERSE 1: 0:12.8–0:25.6 (bars 9–16)

**S04 · 12.8–16.0 · [match: the half-sheet in her hands]** CLOSE on ChatGPT (3/4).
- **Event:** her eyes scan the paper in saccades, and faint teal crease lines ghost onto the sheet a moment *before* she folds them. She sees the structure first. Then her hands blur into a folding flurry: four fold stages, each with a smear and a pizzicato. A needle-nosed dart is done at 15.2. She lifts it and her eyes gleam.
- **Camera:** an eased snap-zoom (with a small overshoot) onto her eyes at 13.0, then a pull back to the hands.
- **Reads:**
  - 12.8–13.8: she's looking at the structure
  - 13.8–15.2: fast, precise folding
  - 15.2–16.0: done, and pleased

**S05 · 16.0–19.2 · [cut on the throw's anticipation]** WIDE, rooftop.
- **Event:** she coils, with the tail winding the opposite way, then whips: the dart shoots off screen-right. The camera whip-pans after it with a smear. The dart pulls up far too steeply, loops, and comes back; the camera whips back with it. **Thunk (18.4): it spears onto her horn.** She freezes. Her eyes roll up to look at it. Her tail droops, then gives a small flick. She is self-amused, not angry.
- **Camera:** two whip pans (eased, with follow lag and a 2-frame overshoot), then settling on her.
- **Reads:**
  - 16.0–16.4: the throw
  - 16.4–17.6: the dart flies, loops and returns
  - 18.4–19.2: it's stuck on her horn, and she's embarrassed

**S06 · 19.2–22.4 · [the camera keeps panning right from S05, onto Claude]** MEDIUM on Claude.
- **Event:** Claude has watched the whole thing, deadpan. She folds slowly, pressing each crease twice with her thumb. **Insert (20.8–21.6):** she folds a small star into the nose of her plane, lit amber in the late sun. Her hair barely moves.
- **Camera:** a very slow push-in.
- **Reads:**
  - 19.2–20.6: she's slow and careful
  - 20.8–21.6: the star in the nose (plant; it pays off in S14, S20, S32)
  - 21.6–22.4: a finished, neat plane

**S07 · 22.4–25.6 · [cut on the release]** WIDE (same setup as S05).
- **Event:** a minimal wind-up and a graceful release. The plane glides in a beautiful slow circle… and settles softly into the rain gutter a metre away (24.8). Hold. ChatGPT, with the dart still on her horn, bursts out laughing and covers her mouth. Claude gives her a side-eye, and one corner of her mouth twitches by about 1 px.
- **Camera:** locked, with a slight pan to follow the glide.
- **Reads:**
  - 22.4–24.8: a lovely flight that goes nowhere
  - 24.8–25.6: ChatGPT's laugh, and Claude's dry side-eye

## PRE-CHORUS 1: 0:25.6–0:32.0 (bars 17–20)

**S08 · 25.6–27.2 · [match on the gutter]** CLOSE on the gutter.
- **Event:** ChatGPT's tail tip snakes into frame, hooks Claude's plane and flips it up out of frame.
- **Reads:** the tail is *prehensile*, and it's clever.

**S09 · 27.2–28.8 · [split-screen panels slide in]** TWO STACKED PANELS.
- **Top:** ChatGPT's eyes dart over the plane, lock on the star-keel, and her pupils tighten. That's the "click", with a tiny smile.
- **Bottom (starts 0.4 s later):** Claude's eyes on ChatGPT's hands, steady.
- **Reads:** she understands the star; Claude is watching her understand it.

**S10 · 28.8–32.0 · [panels slide apart onto a two-shot]** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT.
- **Event:** two lightning pinches add winglets (28.8 and 29.0). ChatGPT holds the plane out *by its tail*. Claude looks at the plane, then at her. Hesitation (0.5 s). She takes the nose. Both are holding it. They lean back together in the wind-up: tail and hair swing back (anticipation for the downbeat).
- **Reads:**
  - 28.8–29.4: the improvement
  - 29.4–30.8: the offer and the hesitation
  - 30.8–32.0: holding it together, and winding up

## CHORUS 1: 0:32.0–0:44.8 (bars 21–28)

**S11 · 32.0–35.2 · [cut on the throw at the downbeat]** LOW ANGLE, from behind them.
- **Event:** the joint throw. Two arms extend, and the plane leaves four fingertips on one frame. It climbs straight and true over the roofs. They become silhouettes against the gold sky, the tail and hair streaming.
- **Camera:** an eased crane up following the plane.
- **Reads:** it flies, *together*.

**S12 · 35.2–38.4 · [camera carries with the plane]** FLYING, tracking beside the plane over the town (multiplane parallax).
- **Event:** it skims a laundry line. A sheet lifts off and **folds itself in mid-air** into a second plane (35.2). The two skim two more loose sheets (36.0) and become four; then eight (36.8), on the "again"s.
- **Reads:**
  - 35.2–36.0: *it made a copy*
  - 36.0–37.6: doubling, on the beat

**S13 · 38.4–41.6 · [flock wipe: the eight planes cross the lens]** WIDE, from the front.
- **Event:** the eight swoop back past the pair in formation. Their wake lifts Claude's hair and ChatGPT's coat. ChatGPT grabs Claude's sleeve in excitement.
- **Reads:** they came back; ChatGPT is delighted.

**S14 · 41.6–44.8 · [cut in on the axis]** MEDIUM.
- **Event:** Claude looks down at the hand on her sleeve, then at ChatGPT. ChatGPT notices, lets go and tucks her hands away, sheepish, with a quick tail curl. A plane passes close: **the amber star is on its nose**, and Claude's eyes catch it. "…and we've done three."
- **Reads:** the sleeve beat; the star is still there.

## POST-CHORUS: 0:44.8–0:48.0 (bars 29–30)

**S15 · 44.8–48.0 · [the camera pans with the planes]** From the ridge.
- **Event:** the eight planes peel off and dive over the roof edge into the town. The camera tilts down after them and the frame **creases and folds over** (paper-fold transition #1) onto…

## VERSE 2: 0:48.0–1:00.8 (bars 31–38), sunset rose

**S16 · 48.0–49.6** A bench.
- **Event:** a man reading a newspaper. The paper is yanked from his hands and folds into a plane as it rises. His hands stay in reading position. He blinks.
- **Out:** a whip pan following the paper plane.

**S17 · 49.6–51.2** The grocer's door.
- **Event:** a receipt roll unspools out the door and folds into a chain of little planes. The grocer laughs, hands on hips. A page of sheet music leaves an upstairs window.
- **Out:** a match cut on a receipt plane.

**S18 · 51.2–54.4 · [match: receipt plane → sleeker plane]** TRACKING alongside the flock.
- **Event:** successive generations pass the lens, each one sleeker. At 52.8 one plane's wings **begin to beat**: it's a bird. In the background, ChatGPT runs along the roof ridge, keeping pace, tail streaming, delighted.
- **Reads:** better every copy; then *they flap*; ChatGPT runs with them.

**S19 · 54.4–57.6 · [ChatGPT runs out of frame-right; the camera settles on Claude standing still]** HIGH ANGLE, Claude's point of view down to the street.
- **Seen:** faces turning up; a child running after the birds; an old man holding his hat, his expression mixed.
- **Camera:** a slow push down.
- **Reads:** the people see it; one is thrilled, one isn't sure.

**S20 · 57.6–60.8 · [reverse shot onto Claude's eyes]** CLOSE on Claude.
- **Event:** a bird passes close, and her eyes track it: the star is on its breast. A tiny exhale. Another bird: the star. Another: the star. Her eyes track a little faster each time, as the pace outruns her. "It's still there. It's still there."
- **Out:** flock wipe.

## PRE-CHORUS 2: 1:00.8–1:07.2 (bars 39–42), dusk violet

**S21 · 60.8–64.0** WIDE, the town.
- **Event:** paper pours upward from everywhere: the bookshop door, the school windows, and at 64.0 a drawer that bursts open and lets a thick instruction manual flap away (absurd). The flocks join together.
- **Camera:** a pan up toward the roofs.

**S22 · 64.0–67.2 · [pan continues up to the roofs]** WIDE, the rooftops.
- **Event:** ChatGPT leaps roof to roof toward the water tower. It's a big jump: anticipation, stretch, an arc, then a landing with the tail's follow-through. Claude stays on the original roof, small in the frame, watching her go.

## CHORUS 2: 1:07.2–1:20.0 (bars 43–50), night falls and the Moon rises over the sea

**S23 · 67.2–70.4 · [cut on the landing at the downbeat]** LOW ANGLE, ChatGPT on the water tower.
- **Event:** arms wide, she "conducts" the flock, and it swirls around her in doubling spirals. Pure joy.

**S24 · 70.4–73.6 · [crane up with the spiral]** EXTREME WIDE.
- **Event:** the flock **doubles on every beat** and forms a spiral column climbing from the town toward the rising Moon. The windows of the town light up one by one: people watching.

**S25 · 73.6–76.8 · [tilt down the column to the roofs]** TWO-LEVEL COMPOSITION.
- **Seen:** in the foreground, Claude on her roof. She is looking **down at the lit windows**, not at the flock. In the background, ChatGPT tiny on the tower, silhouetted against the column. (Subtext: the song cheers "Up there!"; Claude's attention is elsewhere.)

**S26 · 76.8–80.0 · [pull up to the Moon]** The Moon.
- **Event:** the column's tip touches the Moon (78.4, "we're at forty!"). A ripple. The flock wraps into a coil around the Moon ("…forty-one").

## BRIDGE: 1:20.0–1:32.8 (bars 51–58), night

**S27 · 80.0–86.4 · [match on the closing coil]** INTERCUT: the wide coil and ChatGPT on the tower.
- **Event:** she flicks her tail left, and the coil swings left a moment later (a graphic match cut from tail to coil). Right: it follows. She spins: it spins. She laughs.

**S28 · 86.4–89.6** ChatGPT, medium.
- **Event:** "…left?" She flicks. **The coil does not follow.** The music drops out. Her tail freezes mid-flick and her eyes dart: for the first time she has no answer.
- **Out:** a smash cut to quiet.

**S29 · 89.6–92.8 · [cut to silence]** WIDE.
- **Event:** the coil slowly turns its centre, with the Moon behind it like an eye, **toward the town and toward them**. ChatGPT drops quietly from the tower and lands soft on the roof beside Claude. The fast one retreats to the careful one. Claude looks up: "Is it still there?"

## BREAKDOWN: 1:32.8–1:45.6 (bars 59–66), solo piano

**S30 · 92.8–99.2** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT from behind, both on the ridge looking up.
- **Event:** ChatGPT's tail lowers slowly and curls around her own leg (anxiety). Claude glances at it. She takes one step sideways and closes the gap; her hair follows and settles. The tail uncurls from ChatGPT's leg and, without her looking, finds **Claude's ankle** and wraps it loosely (96.0). Claude looks down at it. She doesn't move away. She looks back up.
- **Reads:**
  - 92.8–94.4: the tail curls around her own leg
  - 94.4–95.2: Claude notices
  - 95.2–96.0: the step
  - 96.0–97.6: the tail on the ankle
  - 97.6–99.2: Claude doesn't move away

**S31 · 99.2–105.6 · [tilt up from the ankle to the sky]** From below.
- **Event:** a single bird detaches from the coil and spirals down. As it descends it **refolds into the first plane's shape**: winglets and a star-keel. Claude slowly raises her open palm and **waits**, completely still. ChatGPT watches the palm. The plane lands on it perfectly (104.0).

## FINAL CHORUS: 1:45.6–1:58.4 (bars 67–74), moonlight

**S32 · 105.6–108.8 · [cut on the catch]** CLOSE on the hands.
- **Event:** together they unfold it, each holding two corners, which rhymes with S02. At the centre: **the amber star crease**. ChatGPT exhales and her tail loosens.

**S33 · 108.8–112.0 · [tilt up from the sheet]** CLOSE TWO-SHOT, faces lit from below by the paper.
- **Event:** ChatGPT looks at Claude. Claude lets **one small smile** show, in the eyes more than the mouth. It's the warmest moment in the film, and it's tiny.

**S34 · 112.0–115.2 · [tilt down]** INSERT, the sheet.
- **Event:** **a new crease begins drawing itself** across the sheet, a jade-and-amber line that neither of them made. Both look at it. "Where's it going?" "I don't know." "…Let's see."

**S35 · 115.2–118.4 · [tilt up with their gaze]** From behind and below them.
- **Event:** the coil in the sky begins to **unfold**: facets opening, light leaking from its seams. ChatGPT's eyes light up with curiosity, reborn. "Forty-two folds to the moon —" **Bar 74 (116.8) is silent**: the crease completes on the sheet, and the sky's first seam opens without a sound.

## OUTRO: 1:58.4–2:04.8 (bars 75–78)

**S36 · 118.4–121.6 · [pull back]** WIDE from behind.
- **Seen:** two small figures on the roof, the tail around the ankle, the sky opening above them.
- **Event:** jade and amber light floods the frame and becomes white.

**S37 · 121.6–124.8 · [white becomes paper]** The sheet on the roof tiles, the same framing as S02.
- **Event:** two creases now: a star, and one line nobody drew. The wind lifts a corner. At **123.2** the frame itself folds shut along the new crease, on the paper-fold sound. Paper. End.

**[transition out: the 42nd fold]**

## Checklist against the rules

- [x] An event in every shot. Nothing is "two characters standing".
- [x] Reads are sequential, and cause comes before reaction (tear → offer → take; spear → laugh; no-follow → freeze → retreat).
- [x] Every seam has a named transition.
- [x] No text anywhere: the lyrics are never shown, and the numbers are only sung.
- [x] The ending rhymes with the opening (S02 / S37).
- [x] One world: one rooftop, one town, one sky, one continuous evening.
- [x] Every lyric has a visual, and several deliberately contradict the acting (S25, S28, the final chorus).
