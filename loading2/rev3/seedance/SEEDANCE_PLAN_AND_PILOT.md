### Acceptance criteria (every pilot clip)

1. **Identity matches the identity sheet**:
   - two dark horns with teal tips, the same size and curve in every clip;
   - black hair with teal ends;
   - the crossed teal clip;
   - teal eyes;
   - dark coat, white shirt, dark teal tie or ribbon;
   - no new accessories.
2. **Anatomy:**
   - five fingers;
   - consistent hand size;
   - no merging or extra joints;
   - the face does not distort during motion;
   - the elbow, wrist and shoulder move plausibly.
3. **Motion reads:**
   - there is anticipation or ease-in and a settle;
   - hair follows the head with lag;
   - no jitter, flicker or morphing between frames.
4. **The light obeys one path:**
   - it enters top-right and moves down-left;
   - there is one small orb, with no extra light sources;
   - it lights her hand and face as it nears (S2/S3);
   - contact reads in S3: the palm gives and recovers.
5. **Camera is locked** (≤ 2% drift). No cuts, zoom punches or re-framing inside a clip.
6. **Style:**
   - 2D anime cel shading consistent with the references, not 3D or photoreal;
   - night palette;
   - no text or watermark.
7. **Technical:**
   - covers the usable range plus ≥ 0.5 s of handle each side;
   - ≥ 1280×720, 16:9 (1920×1080 preferred);
   - 24 fps preferred. 25 or 30 fps footage is conformed by frame selection; the music is never
     retimed.
   - No speed change above 10% in the edit. If more is needed, regenerate.

**Placement rule.** The editor places the chosen frames at the song times above. Handles are
trimmed, never compressed. A clip that cannot fit its window without a > 10% speed change fails.
