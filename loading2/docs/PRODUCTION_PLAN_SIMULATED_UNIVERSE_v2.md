## 1. Continuity: one coherent state map

### 1.1 The problem in the previous plan

- Old S12 flattened the girls ("they settle as intact contours on the plane").
- Old S13 had them floating with "torso leads, legs follow". That is volumetric again, with no event
  that restores them.
- Old S21 flattened them again ("the dimensional rule reaches the travellers themselves"), with no
  explanation.

### 1.2 Proposal A (recommended; staging proposal, not a locked reading)

**The world flattens. The girls stay on the surviving boundary, follow it outward, and become part of
the artwork only near the climax, by choice.**

| span | world | girls | place | why (lyric) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01–S13 (0:00–1:36) | volumetric (W3) | volumetric (V) | parapet | establishes depth, heat and the trace |
| S14 (1:36.0) | flattens far to near; the parapet rim survives | V, standing on the rim | rim | "everything fell flat, three dimensions into two, **and still I looked at that**": the observer survives the event and looks at the flattened world |
| S15 | the flat plane rises toward them | V, walking | rim, curving upward | "the flat came near, **we followed it upward** to the edge of the sphere" |
| S16 | flat world below | V, floating | starlight path | "we flew out past the stars" |
| S17–S23 | room; flat world seen through the window | V | backstage room | "a room at the end of the light…" |
| S24 (2:50.3) | the window artwork (W2) | **V → C (drawn contours), the first and only time** | inside the art | "**And one day we'll be paper**, and we'll still be in the art": future tense, so they are not paper before this |
| S25 (3:00.0) | the drawing regains depth | C → V | back to the parapet | "maybe death is only a **return**": the one return |
| S26–S30 | W3, the city partly reloaded | V | parapet | "death is only a return… still loading" |

**Lyric fit.**
- "and still I looked at that" needs an observer outside the flattened image.
- "one day we'll be paper" is in the future tense, so the girls cannot already be paper at 1:36.
- Proposal A reads "everything fell flat" as everything in the *world*. This is the one stretch in
  the reading, and the owner should decide it.

**Alternatives (not chosen; listed for the owner's decision):**
- **B. The girls flatten with everything at S14 and re-inflate at the edge of the sphere (S16).**
  - Literal "everything".
  - It needs a second, unmotivated event to restore them; the lyrics give none.
  - It makes S24 "one day we'll be paper" a repeat of something that already happened.
- **C. The girls stay 2D from S14 until the return at S25.**
  - Literal, and drawn-contour acting may be cheaper to animate.
  - It contradicts the future tense of "one day we'll be paper".
  - It removes volumetric acting from the whole bridge (room, keys, lanterns), where the girls'
    reactions carry the story.

### 1.5 The climax is built from human traces and the girls' responses

There is no cosmic background montage.

- **S20 (2:22.8) "But look who's pressing the keys."** Keys go down under the warm footprints of
  passing travellers, so the keys are pressed by passers-by, not by a god or by the girls.
- **S21–S22.** A lights her lantern from a footstep, and both take two steps along the lit traces.
- **S23 (break, 2:43.5–2:50.3).** They lift their lanterns to the window. The flat world is made of
  every trace already shown:
  - the letter, the cradle and the parting hands (S04);
  - the first fire (S06);
  - the signal (S11);
  - the footsteps (S20–S21).

  One familiar light goes out, but its mark stays. B's hand reaches the glass.
- **S24.** They step into the art, holding hands.
- **S25–S26.** The one return, then the outro S27–S30. The dimensional idea is told by the note in
  B's hands (two, three, one), not by another cosmic montage.

### 2.3 Unresolved items (kept open, not deleted)

**D1. The four main Final Chorus lines (F01–F04).** The author text has: "Where we live is a
simulated universe, / and the thing that came down from the edge of the sky / was every traveler who
ever passed through here, / every light that went out and never said goodbye."

What the audio shows between "and not I" (ends 163.1) and "And one day" (170.3), in the top panel of
the evidence figure:
- 163.5–164.5: near silence;
- 165.0–168.0: one held high voice at about 600 Hz;
- harmonics continue to about 171.2.

ASR in those windows returns "Oh", "I", and "*Epic Music*", and no words from F01–F04.

ASR missing a line is not proof the line is absent. Held sung vowels and choir layers often defeat
ASR. There is a timing argument, though, and it is a measurement, not a hearing:
- Chorus 1 lines take about 3.5–4 s each (52.4 → 56.3 → 59.3 → 63.0).
- Four such lines need about 14–16 s.
- The only vocal window before "And one day" is about 7 s, and it contains one sustained vowel.

The lines could still be present elsewhere, for example buried as a backing layer.

**Status: UNRESOLVED.** S23 keeps the four lines on screen in the animatic, marked unresolved, with
the evidence panel. If someone listening finds them, S23 and S24 are retimed to them. If not, S23
stays wordless.
