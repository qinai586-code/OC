# LOADING (light version) — direction

The whole film is made of light. There are no painted backgrounds. The only illustrated
things are A (ChatGPT) and B (Claude), cut out from their character images. Everything else
is light points, lines, planes and spheres, built and moved in real 3D.

The aesthetic follows `opening_v5`: black space, warm human lights, a thin horizon line, the
two witnesses small against it, close-ups lit by the scene's own light, constellations drawn
as lines.

## One idea

The song counts down through dimensions and never reaches zero. **3, 2, 1… Loading.** A world
is rendered from people's light, flattened by something vast, returned, and is still loading.

## The vocabulary (and nothing else)

| element | meaning |
|---|---|
| **point** (warm amber) | one human life, one act, one light. Cities are real: 7,300 populated places from Natural Earth, each lit in proportion to its population |
| **line** | a path between lights: road → footstep trail → constellation |
| **plane** | 2D: the map, the flattened world, paper |
| **sphere** | 3D: the Earth, the light cone, the wall |
| **lattice** | the simulation: laws, the grid that does not change |
| **red** | the countdown, the threat (used three times only) |
| **teal / amber** | A / B when they become light |

Dimensional changes are geometry, never wipes: a sphere unrolls into a plane, a plane turns
edge-on into a line, a line contracts to a point, and back.

## Lyric meanings (sources in the research list)

- *signals into the night* — the Arecibo message, humanity's real 1974 transmission:
  1,679 bits (23 × 73). It leaves a city as 1,679 points and assembles into its bitmap in the sky.
- *dark forest* — Liu Cixin's answer to the Fermi paradox: every civilisation hides its light.
  The forest is drawn as thin vertical lines; the stars hold still; nothing answers.
- *a countdown burned red at the back of your eyes* — the sophon countdown printed on Wang
  Miao's retina in *The Three-Body Problem*: a ring of red tick marks over an eye, ticking down.
- *a card from its sleeve / a sheet thinner than any mind could believe / everything fell
  flat* — the dual-vector foil of *Death's End*: edge-on it is invisible; on contact one
  dimension curls up, and everything collapses into a plane, preserved in full detail as it
  spreads out like a vast painting.
- *the speed of light is a wall* — the light cone: everything you can ever reach is inside an
  expanding shell of light.
- *Planck scale is fog* — resolution ends: points dissolve into jitter.
- *death is only a return* — the Returners of *Death's End*: mass is given back so the
  universe can begin again. Every particle flies home and the sphere re-forms.
- *travellers with lanterns* — tōrō nagashi: lights carried through the dark to guide people
  home; footsteps become paths, paths become constellations.

## Rules (from the brief)

- Every particle has an origin, a path and a meaning, and moves in the scene's depth.
- Every camera move reveals scale, information, an eyeline or a change of dimension.
- One shot makes the next: point → line → plane → sphere and back; light → trace → gaze →
  constellation; city → road → footstep → constellation.
- A looks outward (up, at scale); B looks back (down, at people).
- Cuts land on phrases, breaths and held notes; silence is held as stillness.
