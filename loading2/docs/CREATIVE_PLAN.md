# LOADING — redesigned creative plan (light version)

## 1. What the references show (measured)

The two Claude MVs (V1 retro cel, V2 world of light) are measured in `REFERENCE_ANALYSIS.md`.
V2 is the model for movement: one continuous camera, transitions made of particles and light,
viseme lip-sync, and lyrics typed in light as they are sung. **This film has no hard cuts** (§5).


**`opening_v5.mp4` (21 s): the first 21 seconds of this same master** (audio correlation 0.97 at
zero offset). It is the model for this film.

- Cuts land on vocal events: 5.7 ("3", a hard flash cut), 7.6 ("1"), 8.5, 10.3, 12.0, 13.4 (the
  breath), 14.5 (the drop), 17.7, 19.5. Shots run 1.5–3 s, each with one continuous inner move.
- The world is only light: the Earth is its city lights. Sphere → plane → edge-on line → horizon,
  as continuous geometry. The ledge is that line.
- The characters are never pasted static. They assemble out of the particles, and each close-up
  acts: A sings with her mouth in sync with the vocal and blinks; B's close-up has her eyes closed,
  with hair and camera alive. **Each drawing appears once.**
- Close-ups sit on a soft, dark gradient with motivated light shafts and dust, not on black.
- Particles have a source (cities), a path (rising) and a destination (the constellation).

**`preview_720p.mp4` (2:36, pixel MV):** 122 cuts in 156 s (about 1.3 s per shot). Every lyric line
gets its own concrete idea, and abstractions become places (a loss curve they walk on, a network
that lights up, chart axes they stand on). The characters are always doing something (walk and
run cycles, gestures). The look is one consistent style throughout.

## 3. Character rule: no repeated visuals

Each drawing (or acting sequence) is used **once** in the whole film. Inside its shot it always
moves through particle assembly, lip-sync, blinks, expression morphs, breathing, hair and wind.
Everywhere else, A and B are present as **two lights** (teal and amber): two points on the horizon
line, two travellers, the two lights in the room.

| drawing | shot | what it does there |
|---|---|---|
| KV1 pair, seated from behind | C04 | assembles from the city lights onto the line ("born in your traces") |
| KV3 / KV3b heads from behind | C07 | A lifts her head to the new constellation; B stays looking down |
| KV4 B profile | C09 | eyes lower to the pulsing lights; warmth on her face, then it leaves |
| KV5 pair, A's open palm | C11 | the first fire's light travels into A's palm |
| A head "curious" | C14 | sings "what if we live in a simulated universe?"; the stars snap to a grid behind her |
| B heads neutral → look → eyes closed | C17 | looks away and closes her eyes; the unwatched world runs coarse |
| A head "focused" | C21 | extreme close-up on the eye; the red countdown ring ticks in it |
| B head "surprised" | C26 | "and still I looked at that": the flattened world lights her face |
| B head "thinking" | C36 | speaks "It isn't." |
| A and B full figures (front) | C43 | stand in the rising flood of lights on the held "I…", hair lifted by the updraft |
| A and B turnaround sheets | C44 | turn front → 3/4 → side, then edge-on: paper-thin lines of light |
| A head neutral → smile; B head slight smile | C45 | "and we'll still be in the light" |

Lip-sync is driven by the isolated vocal's loudness. The mouth is a new drawn shape (open/mid/closed)
on the front-facing heads. Blinks are quick morphs to the eyes-closed drawings. Every other shot
has no character drawing at all.

## 5. One camera: how every shot becomes the next

There is no hard cut anywhere. The C-numbers are beats of one continuous camera. Every change of
place is a motivated transition, and each transition is also a step in the dimension grammar:
- **gate:** push through a point of light (3D → 0D → 3D);
- **fold:** sphere → plane → line;
- **state:** body or object → particles → body;
- **reveal:** pull out, and the scene was inside something;
- **follow:** the camera rides a particle's path.

|---|---|---|
| C01→C02 | 5.7 | follow: the arriving lights complete the sphere; a ring sweeps the limb on "3" and the camera surges in |
| C02→C03 | 12.6 | fold: the edge-on line contracts into one point |
| C03→C04 | 14.3 | gate: the camera passes through the point on the drop; it opens into the horizon line and the plane below |

**Camera:** always moving, with slow push-ins inside a beat and fast moves only at gates. A
slight roll drift (about 0.5° per second) keeps the frame breathing.
