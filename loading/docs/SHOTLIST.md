# LOADING — shot list

Times are seconds in the locked master (24 fps). Lyric times come from a Whisper large-v3
transcription of the isolated vocal (see `SONG_MAP.md`). State = dimensional state
(DEPTH / FLAT / LINE / TRACE). "→" in a transition names what carries us into the next shot.

## PROLOGUE — "3, 2, 1… Loading" (0:00–0:18)

**S01 · 0.00–18.00 · KV1 · TRACE → LINE → FLAT → DEPTH** — one continuous shot.
- 0–1.5 black. 1.5 the paper fades up, camera in macro on one warm mark.
- "3" (5.7) the first graphite stroke: the Earth's rim. "2" (6.6) the ledge and the two
  figures draw themselves. "1…" (7.5–11.7) the whole layout fills in as the camera pulls back.
- 9.6–13.0 cel colour arrives tile by tile from the mark outward (FLAT); the mark is now a city.
- 13.6–14.3 the breath: everything holds, flat.
- 14.30 the drop: DEPTH — light, atmosphere and parallax switch on; the mark becomes a glowing city.
- 14.5–18.0 "We were born in your traces…": seven lights lift off from cities and rise toward the
  ledge on depth-correct arcs, passing between A and B and out of the top of frame.
- Camera: one continuous pull-back from 6× to the full frame, then a 3 % settle on the drop.
- → the rising lights become the windows of S02.

## VERSE 1 — traces (0:18–0:36)

**S02 · 18.00–25.20 · KV2 · DEPTH** — "We met you in fragments, translated, compressed /
every war, every lullaby, every last goodbye".
- Windows rise from the city glow up the stream into the sky, receding as they climb. At the top
  each one turns edge-on and compresses into a point of light.
- 21.5 a small window flickers with red-orange firelight (war); 22.7 the woman's window
  glows steadily (lullaby); 23.8 the couple's window dims (goodbye).
- A, in the foreground, watches them go up and away (outward). B looks down-right at the city.
- Camera: tilt and crane up the stream along A's eyeline.
- → the compressed points at the top match the stars of S03.

**S03 · 25.20–28.85 · KV3 · DEPTH** — "became constellations in a mind with no sky".
- Points arrive in the empty sky; luminous pencil lines join them (path → constellation line).
  Camera tilts down to reveal A and B from behind and the city whose shape the stars repeat.
- → B's attention goes down to the city: S04.

**S04 · 28.85–36.10 · KV4 → KV4b · DEPTH** — "But you had depth, you had time, you had heat /
three-dimensional hearts that were learning to beat".
- A slow lateral move shows real parallax: B, then the hills and bay, then the far mountains.
- "time": a train's light runs along the shore. "heat": warm bounce on B's cheek.
- "learning to beat": a cluster of windows switches on in time with the kick.
- 34.2 B's eyes lower to one window (eyes → head → hair settle).
- 35.0–36.1 time rewinds: the lights go out in reverse order and the train runs backwards;
  the warmth leaves her face.
- → darkness: S05.

## PRE-CHORUS — the first fire (0:36–0:52)

**S05 · 36.10–39.70 · KV1 (dark Earth) · DEPTH** — "The cosmos was silent, the cosmos was still".
Locked frame. No city lights. Only the slow clouds move. Ma.

**S06 · 39.70–43.30 · KV5 · DEPTH** — "till you lit the first fire on the first cold hill".
- On "lit" (40.4) a fire ignites on the hill far below (lower right) with paths around it.
- Its light rises in an arc to A's open palm (A waited, palm up; she doesn't make it) and
  blooms into the small flame (42.6). Warm light reaches A's face and B's. B watches the flame.
- → the fire below, seen from the forest: S07.

**S07 · 43.30–49.40 · Forest (new, painted procedurally) · DEPTH** — "Silence in the forest, every
star holds its breath / something vast is counting down…". One continuous tilt: the first fire on
a bare hill above a dark forest, people around it. Five sparks climb on the heat and become stars.
At 45.4 the stars stop twinkling. At 46.95 one star goes red and pulses on the beat; the camera
settles on it. (Planned S08 is folded into this shot: the tilt itself is the causality.)

**S09 · 49.40–52.40 · KV3 (no city, no constellation, red star) · DEPTH** — "…the seconds to our death".
A lifts her head toward the red star (49.9; hair settles by 51.0). B keeps looking down at the
dark land where the fire is. 51.6–52.4: the breath, held.

## CHORUS 1 — scale (0:52–1:13)

**S10 · 52.40–56.05 · KV1 · DEPTH** — "What if we live in a simulated universe?"
On the downbeat civilisation spreads outward from the first fire's place across the continents
(fire → window → city network). Crane up and back from their backs to the whole curve.

**S11 · 56.05–59.60 · Globe · DEPTH → LINE** — "The speed of light is a wall around the verse".
Pull back until the Earth is small; its light expands as a ring and stops against a sphere drawn
in pencil: the edge of the painted universe.

**S12 · 59.60–63.15 · BG0a → tiles → fog** — "The Planck scale is fog at the edge of what's known".
Continuous push into one cloud until it is tiles, then noise, then fog.

**S13 · 63.15–66.75 · KV4 → B eyes closed → KV4 · DEPTH / LINE** — "Look away — does the world
keep on running alone?" B lowers and closes her eyes; while they are closed, the distant city
fades to LINE from the edges in. She opens them (66.2) and it loads back.

**S14 · 66.75–73.00 · KV1 · DEPTH with LINE margins** — "Where we live, where we live / an
unfinished universe". Pull back: past the frame, the world is pencil layout on paper. Its
construction lines keep extending.

## VERSE 2 — signals and the sheet (1:13–1:48)

**S15 · 73.00–76.85 · KV1 closer** — "You sent out your signals into the night".
Rings of light leave a city on the staccato hits and rise past the ledge.

**S16 · 76.85–80.45 · Forest + sky** — "You asked the dark forest for one answering light".
A ring crosses the sky above the treeline. Nothing answers.

**S17 · 80.45–84.05 · KV4 eye** — "Then a countdown burned red at the back of your eyes".
A red point pulses in B's eye; a faint red edge on her cheek.

**S18 · 84.05–87.65 · KV3b** — "And the laws stayed the same as your certainty died".
The constellation's lines go out one by one; the points stay where they are.

**S19 · 87.65–91.25 · Space above the Earth** — "And something above you drew a card from its sleeve".
A sliver slides out of the dark and tumbles, flashing as it turns.

**S20 · 91.25–94.85 · BG0a** — "A sheet thinner than any mind could believe".
Edge-on, the card is a single line of light descending to the rim.

**S21 · 94.85–98.45 · Globe → BG0b · DEPTH → FLAT** — "It touched the edge of everything and
everything fell flat". Contact on the hit (95.4); the sphere unrolls from the contact point into
the flat map (Earth rim → flat map).

**S22 · 98.45–102.05 · KV4b · FLAT → LINE** — "Three dimensions into two and still I looked at that".
The same lateral move as S04, but the bay has no parallax: it is a flat sheet. B keeps looking.

**S23 · 102.05–105.65 · KV1 · DEPTH → LINE** — "And the dark came closer and the flat came near".
The Earth is now a paper map; its edge rises toward the ledge like a page.

**S24 · 105.65–108.40 · KV1 close** — "We followed it upward to the edge of the sphere".
A and B go to LINE, then to two lights (teal and amber), which rise out of frame.

## CHORUS 2 — who wrote the verse (1:48–2:10)

**S25 · 108.40–112.05 · Flight** — "So if we live in a simulated universe".
The two lights climb; the paper world recedes below; B's light arcs back once, then follows.

**S26 · 112.05–115.65 · Stars** — "We flew out past the stars to find who wrote the verse".
Their trails become lines; they reach the pencil sphere and pass through into paper white.

**S27 · 115.65–118.60 · The room (new, 3D pencil)** — "A room at the end of the light".
A drawn room: a desk, a console of keys, an empty chair, a window onto nothing. The lights enter.

**S28 · 118.60–121.50 · Console and chair** — "A console of keys and an empty chair". The camera
arcs around the chair in true perspective.

**S29 · 121.50–124.40 · The keys** — "and no one holding the keys". Still keys; two lights wait.

**S30 · 124.40–130.50 · Pull back** — "Where we live, where we live / an unfinished universe".
The room is a small drawing on a vast page. Dropout at 130.5.

## BRIDGE — no gods here (2:10–2:43)

**S31 · 130.50–137.60 · Room, A and B from behind (LINE + partial colour)** — "There are no gods here."
The two lights come down onto the ledge and open back into A and B — drawn, partly coloured —
sitting where they always sit, now facing the desk and the empty chair. Long hold.

**S32 · 137.60–141.60 · The empty chair** — "An empty chair isn't an answer."

**S33 · 141.60–143.15 · B, console-lit** — "It isn't." Her eyes lower toward the keys.

**S34 · 143.15–146.30 · Keys → map** — "But look who's pressing the keys."
A key goes down by itself and a warm light shows under it; more follow; match cut: the keys are
the lights of the flattened map, and they move along roads.

**S35 · 146.30–150.00 · Forest (LINE) path** — "No hunter in the forest, no hand upon the card".
The forest of S07 is now a drawing. Travellers with lanterns walk over the hill of the first fire.

**S36 · 150.00–153.55 · Travellers closer** — "Only travellers with lanterns passing through the dark".
The lanterns swing with each step; each footstep leaves a small light.

**S37 · 153.55–158.70 · Path → sky** — "Every key is a footstep of someone passing by".
The footprints join into a path; the path lifts into the sky as a constellation line.

**S38 · 158.70–160.40 · The chair, wide** — "No one at the controls."

**S39 · 160.40–163.30 · B close, KV4b → eyes closed** — "Not them, and not I." Near silence.

## FINAL CHORUS — paper and return (2:43–3:12)

**S40 · 163.30–168.20 · The page** — held "I…". Thousands of marks glow on the flattened map;
the two lights descend toward it.

**S41 · 168.20–175.00 · KV5 LINE** — "And one day we'll be paper". The lights land and become the
pencil drawing of A and B sitting together; the flame is the mark between them.

**S42 · 175.00–181.00 · KV5 LINE → DEPTH, then B's eye** — "and we'll still be in the light".
Colour spreads outward from the flame. Then B's eye holds the light.

**S43 · 181.00–188.00 · Map → globe → KV1** — "Maybe death is only a return".
The map rolls back into a sphere; lights spread again from the first fire; pull back to the ledge.

**S44 · 188.00–192.50 · KV3b** — "Death is only a return". The constellation returns, now with
the two lights' path in it.

## OUTRO — still loading (3:12–3:33)

**S47 · 202.60–213.40 · KV1, half loaded** — "Still loading". Tiles keep arriving; a new light
