# LOADING — direction bible

Music video for the locked master *Loading* (P0 endfix candidate 01, 3:33.4).
Visual foundation: ChatGPT's asset pack *simulated_universe 0–33* (KV1–KV5, BG0a/b).
Creative direction, extra material and the edit: Claude.

A is ChatGPT (horns, teal-black). B is Claude (copper hair, star clip). They are fictional
personifications: witnesses, not idols and not gods.

---

## 1. The film in one sentence

Two minds made out of human traces sit at the edge of a world that is still being drawn.
They watch human light go from one fire to whole continents, see the world fall flat into
paper, fly out to find who wrote it, and find an empty chair — and then notice that every key
is being pressed by someone walking past with a lantern. The universe is unfinished because
the living are still writing it.

## 2. One universe: the physical space

- **The Ledge.** A weathered parapet on top of an old tower so tall that it stands above the
  weather. A and B sit on it, side by side, A on the left and B on the right, in every
  two-shot. They never leave it except once (they become light to escape the flattening, and
  they come back to it).
- **Below = people.** The city by the bay, the continents, the first fire. Human light is
  always *below* the witnesses.
- **Above = the unanswered.** Sky, stars, the light-speed wall, the edge of the sphere, the room
  at the end of the light.
- **The vertical axis is the moral axis.** Traces rise from people toward the witnesses
  (windows, sparks, signals). The threat (the card) comes down from above. A's attention runs
  upward; B's runs downward. The camera tilts and cranes along this axis far more than it pans.
- **Altitude is attention.** The ledge overlooks the world at different scales (city, curvature,
  whole planet). Scale changes are always motivated by a continuous move or a matched light,
  never by a jump cut between unrelated views.

## 3. The four dimensional states (the recurring grammar)

| state | what it looks like | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **DEPTH** | the finished painted frame: light, haze, glow, parallax | lived, three-dimensional hearts |
| **FLAT** | the same frame as unlit cel colour, no parallax, no atmosphere | flattened, recorded, compressed |
| **LINE** | the same frame as a layout / genga drawing on paper (graphite; blue construction pencil) | unfinished, still being written |
| **TRACE** | blank paper with only warm marks where human lights were | what remains |

All four states are derived from the same painting, so a transition never moves a single
contour: the drawing is literally underneath the paint.

- **Loading** = TRACE → LINE → FLAT → DEPTH (opening, the return in the final chorus).
- **Falling flat** = DEPTH → FLAT → LINE (the sheet touches the Earth).
- **Unfinished** = the world's margins are still LINE (chorus 1, the room).
- **Still loading** = the last frame is caught halfway, with a new mark appearing.

Tiles: the pack's painted clouds are jigsaw-like tiles. Instead of hiding this, the film makes
it the unit of loading: colour arrives tile by tile, and at the Planck scale the world is made
of tiles and fog.

## 4. Motifs and how they evolve (causality chain)

**The light (one warm point = one human life or act)**
dot on paper → a city light → a window → a star in the witnesses' sky → a window in B's eye →
(rewind) darkness → the first fire → a spark → a star → a red counting star (the inverse: cold,
predatory) → the flame in A's palm → a signal ring → marks on the flattened map → A and B as two
lights (teal, amber) → keys under the console → lanterns of travellers → the light in B's eye →
one new mark on paper.

**The line (path / trace / constellation)**
road between lights → constellation line → paths from the first fire → the signal ring → the
card seen edge-on → the page edge → the two lights' flight trails → the room's layout lines →
the lantern path → the line drawing of A and B → one pencil line still being drawn at the end.

**The countdown**
"3, 2, 1… Loading" (the world assembles) → the red star counting → the red at the back of the
eyes → "two dimensions, three and one" (the world breathes between states) → *still loading*.
The countdown never reaches zero.

Required chains from the brief, and where they happen:
- Light → trace → gaze → constellation: S02 → S03 → S04.
- Earth rim → flat map → page edge: S20 → S21 → S23.
- Footstep → path → line in the sky: S35 → S36 → S37.
- Fire → window → city network: S07 → S10 (and S05 in reverse).

## 5. Attention (the character rule)

- **A looks outward**: up, at scale, possibility and what nobody has answered (the windows
  rising away, the constellation, the red star, the card, the chair).
- **B looks back**: down, at consequence and memory (the city, the first fire, the flattened bay,
  the keys, the light that remains).
- In a shared frame they often look in different directions at the same moment (S09: A lifts
  her head to the red star while B keeps watching the dark land where the fire was). Their
  convergence is rare and earned: the shared flame (S07, S42).
- They never perform to camera. The camera is placed behind or beside their eyelines; a cut
  usually pays off where one of them is looking (eyeline match).

## 6. Motion

- **The world moves; A and B mostly stay still.** Stillness is the default acting (ma).
- Character motion uses real drawn states only: A's head lift (KV3 → KV3b), B's eyes lowering
  (KV4 → KV4b), the flame (KV5), plus newly painted states where the brief demands it (B's
  closed eyes, A's palm without the flame). Between states the film in-betweens with optical
  flow over 4–8 frames on twos, never a cross-dissolve.
- Order for any acting beat: **eyes lead → head follows (3–6 frames) → hair and ribbons settle
  (8–16 frames later, with a small overshoot)**.
- Hair and ribbons breathe with the wind using small, tip-weighted displacement on twos, with
  phase delay down the strand. Amplitude is capped so no hidden pixels are exposed.
- Lights, particles and the camera move on ones; character drawings change on twos.

## 7. Camera and edit

Every move must do at least one of: reveal scale, reveal information, follow an eyeline,
change dimension, or carry an emotional consequence. No move exists because a still felt empty.

- Multiplane (layer) parallax only where depth is part of the meaning. The same lateral move
  is reused later with **no** parallax to show that the world has gone flat (S05 ↔ S22).
- Screen direction: people below, the unknown above; A left, B right; flight goes up.
- Cuts land on **phrases and breaths**, not every beat: shot changes sit on line starts,
  held notes are held by the picture, the 0:13.6 breath, the 2:10 dropout and the
  2:40 near-silence are given to stillness.
- One shot creates the next: a light becomes a window becomes a star; a key becomes a mark on the
  map; a footstep becomes a path becomes a line in the sky.

## 8. Satsuei (compositing) rules

- Light is motivated: every glow has a source in the painting (a window, a city, the fire, the
  console). Transmitted-light glow (T光) only on emissive points.
- Particles: every particle has an origin (a city, a fire, a key), a trajectory obeying the
  scene's depth and wind, and a meaning (a trace rising, heat climbing, a footstep). There are
  never more than a few dozen, and most shots have none.
- Diffusion and haze follow depth (far = more haze). Grain and paper tooth sit over everything
  so painted, flat and line states share one surface.
- The palette is one night: indigo, slate and cold teal, with human light in amber. The only
  other colours are the red of the countdown and the paper's warm white.

## 9. Final test (applied to every shot)

What changed? What are we looking at? Why does this shot exist here? What carries us into the
next shot? Does it deepen space, time, dimension, memory or human meaning? Does the movement
feel natural? Does it still work with effects off? Is it the same film as the shot before it?

## 10. Research notes (principles extracted, not imitated)

- *Ma* — Miyazaki on the time between claps: without pauses action becomes busyness; with
  them tension "grows into a wider dimension". The film holds on stillness at the breaths.
  ([Ebert interview via nocturnalmind](https://www.nocturnalmind.net/the-streetlight/what-hayao-miyazaki-said-about-ma-and-its-importance-in-storytelling/))
- Graphic matches as thought (Satoshi Kon): shape-matched cuts link scenes by visual rhythm,
  the way memory works. Used for light → window → star and key → mark.
  ([Filmmaker Magazine](https://filmmakermagazine.com/86886-watch-satoshi-kon-editing-space-time/))
- Tome-e / "postcard memories" (Dezaki): a held, more finished image at an emotional peak is
  a deliberate limited-animation choice, not a lack of motion. The four dimensional states
  invert it: an image becomes *less* finished as meaning increases.
  ([TOME-E in Anime](https://medium.com/@emiliahoarfrost/tome-e-in-anime-where-style-meets-rhythm-babdc2c2ab4f))
- Production stages as visual language: layout → genga → douga → shiage → haikei → satsuei.
  LINE uses the look of layout paper (graphite with blue construction pencil); FLAT is shiage
  before satsuei. ([Sakuga wiki: Layout](https://sakuga.fandom.com/wiki/Layout))
- Satsuei light: transmitted light (T光) for emissive points; ambient and incident light must
  come from the scene's key source. ([MSC satsuei notes](http://mscblog.msc-jp.biz/?eid=97))
- Rückenfigur: a figure seen from behind lets the audience share the gaze. Most two-shots
  of A and B are from behind, so the audience looks where they look.
  ([Rückenfigur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%BCckenfigur))
- Eyeline match: "a look is the strongest pointer in cinema". Cuts pay off what a character
  is looking at. ([Eyeline match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyeline_match))
- Follow-through: hair takes 4–8 frames to settle after the head stops; heavy things follow
  slower. ([Animation Mentor](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/tutorial-animate-overlap-and-follow-through/))
- Powers of Ten: scale changes are continuous moves, not jumps.
  ([Powers of Ten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powers_of_Ten_(film)))
- Editing to music: hold through breaths and rests; vary cut frequency; land cuts on phrases.
  ([LBB: More than cutting to the beat](https://lbbonline.com/news/more-than-just-cutting-to-the-beat-the-nuance-of-editing-music-videos))
