# Characters and sets: model-sheet brief

This is a hand-off brief for whoever draws the model sheets. **All characters here are new.** None is the user's OC, and none is a personification of an AI product. Story context is in [TREATMENT.md](TREATMENT.md), and every shot is in [STORYBOARD.md](STORYBOARD.md).

## What every sheet needs

- **Style:** soft, clean anime character design: fine line art, gentle cel shading, a muted palette with one signature colour per character. Grounded, everyday clothes: these are New Yorkers, not fantasy characters.
- **Views:** front, 3/4, side and back, full body, with a proportion guide.
- **Expressions:** the ones listed per character, as head close-ups.
- **Palette:** swatches with hex codes.
- **It must survive pixel reduction.** The film is drawn at 320×180. An adult is about 44 px tall and a face about 7 px wide, so each character is identified by **silhouette plus one or two colour blocks**, never by small details. Tell-tale shapes (a bun, a scarf's fringe, a clip) should be big and simple. Close-ups use 2× portraits and hand-drawn eyes derived from these sheets.
- **No logos or text** on clothing or props.

## 1. Iris Lin (林瞳)

**Role:** the protagonist. We see her whole life, from her first frame to her last.
**In one line:** an ordinary girl from Flushing, Queens, whose one habit, turning around fast, never leaves her.
**Signature colour:** iris violet `#7B5CD6`.

**Constants at every age:**
- straight black hair
- dark eyes with a **violet ring** in the iris that catches the light
- a small, plain **violet bar clip** in her hair, worn from age five to eighty
- **the spin:** her signature pose is mid-turn, looking back over her shoulder, with hair (and, from fifteen on, the scarf) flaring out

| Age | Where | Hair | Clothes and props | Sheet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | verse 1 | blunt bob with bangs, violet clip | puffy lilac jacket, striped leggings, sneakers | **full sheet** |
| 8 | pre-chorus 1 | chin length | school cardigan, a book | quick turnaround |
| 11 | pre-chorus 1 | shoulder length | hoodie, sketchbook, pencil behind her ear | quick turnaround |
| 13 | chorus 1 | shoulder length | oversized hoodie, sneakers | quick turnaround |
| 15–16 | verse 2, the climb | long, low ponytail | navy winter coat, **Nai Nai's marigold scarf** from S21 onward, backpack | **full sheet** |
| mid-20s | bridge | shoulder length, loose | light practical jacket, tote bag, scarf | **full sheet** |
| mid-40s | the platform | short bob again (a rhyme with age 5) | dark work coat, work bag, reading glasses pushed up, scarf | **full sheet** |
| 50 | the stoop | bob, gray at the temples | cardigan | quick turnaround |
| 65 | the stoop | silver bob | cardigan | quick turnaround |
| 80 | the stoop, the end | **white hair in a low bun** (a rhyme with Nai Nai) | soft cardigan, still the violet clip. **No scarf:** she has given it to her granddaughter. | **full sheet** |

- **Expressions:**
  - delighted gasp (she has caught the world loading)
  - wide-eyed secret (being told a secret)
  - quiet grief (hospital steps)
  - determined (the climb)
  - stunned stillness (the empty chair)
  - freezing in recognition (the platform)
  - a silent laugh with a finger to her lips (age 80)
  - peace (the end)
- **Motion:** quick, curious head turns all her life. At eighty the turn is small but still fast. Her eyes always move before her head.

## 2. Nai Nai (奶奶)

**Role:** Iris's grandmother. She teaches the rules of the world as a game and is the first to return.
**In one line:** she knows exactly how the world works, treats it as a delicious secret, and never explains more than one sentence.
**Signature colour:** marigold `#F2A93B` (her scarf).

- **Build:** mid-seventies; small and slightly stooped; about 5.5 heads tall.
- **Face:**
  - round, thin-rimmed glasses
  - smile lines
  - eyes that disappear when she laughs
- **Hair:** silver-white, in a low bun with a plain wooden hairpin.
- **Clothes:** a dark plum quilted jacket over a knit sweater, loose dark trousers and cloth shoes.
- **The scarf:** hand-knitted in chunky garter stitch with a short fringe, marigold. It is the one colour that crosses three generations, so it must read at 3–4 px.
- **Props:** a deep-green umbrella (always open one beat before the rain) and knitting needles with marigold wool (S12).
- **Expressions:**
  - a sly secret smile
  - finger to her lips
  - laughing with her eyes closed
  - a soft, calm smile (the hospital)
- **Motion:** slow and economical, and always a beat ahead of the world.

## 3. The Stranger

**Role:** the proof of return. On a late subway platform he hums Nai Nai's tune, which he could not have learned.
**In one line:** a man in his sixties, tired from work, eyes closed, humming to himself.

- **Look:**
  - a worn wool coat and knit beanie
  - gray stubble
  - big hands folded in his lap
  - work boots
- He should be specific and real, not a generic "wise old man."
- **Palette:** browns and grays. His thread is normally a cool warm-white, and it flashes **marigold** for one beat in S36 and again in S43.
- **Expressions:** eyes closed and humming (the only one needed in S35); a small private smile (S43).

## 4. The granddaughter

**Role:** Iris at the start, again (S38–S41).
**In one line:** five years old, fearless, with the marigold scarf wrapped twice and still trailing.

- **Look:** two short pigtails and a mint jacket.
- **The scarf:** Nai Nai's marigold scarf, far too long for her.
- **Design rule:** she should rhyme with Iris at five but be clearly a different child.
- **Expressions:**
  - delighted gasp (the same beat as Iris's in S06)
  - curious look up at the ceiling (S40)
  - listening (S41)
