# Film design: "Where We Live Is a Simulated Universe" (full length, 3:33)

This design follows the rules agreed with ChatGPT: space, time, dimension, attention, human trace, meaning.

- **Visual masters:** the KV frames. Each KV is one intact painted anime frame, never cut-out layers.
- **What moves:** the human world inside the frame, locked local variants for pose changes, and a camera that moves only to follow a trace or an eyeline, or to change scale. No lip-sync.
- **Motif:** warm light is human (city lights, traces, constellation, fire); everything else stays cold. The witnesses watch the transformation; they never cause it.
- **Scale:** each shot sits at human, witness or cosmic scale on purpose. At cosmic scale the girls are small.
- **Dimension as a rhyme:** 3D, 2D and 1D return in new forms, never as the same effect twice. Examples: a globe, then a map, then a line; a road, then a flat path, then a constellation line; bodies, then paper figures, then a line of light.
