# Film design: "Where We Live Is a Simulated Universe" (full length, 3:33)

This design follows the rules agreed with ChatGPT: space, time, dimension, attention, human trace, meaning.

- **Visual masters:** the KV frames. Each KV is one intact painted anime frame, never cut-out layers.
- **What moves:**
  - the human world inside the frame;
  - A's look-up (KV3b, her head only);
  - the paper transition: paint → flat wash → pencil → bare paper, with the human lights surviving as ochre marks;
  - a camera that moves only to follow a trace or an eyeline, or to change scale.

  There is no lip-sync.
- **Frame edges:** every camera view is clamped inside the painting, so no frame edge can show.
- **Motif:** warm light is human (city lights, windows, traces, the constellation, fire); everything else stays cold. The one non-human element, the sheet, is a cold hairline. The witnesses watch the transformations; they never cause them.
- **Scale:** each shot sits at human, witness or cosmic scale on purpose.
- **Dimension as a rhyme:** 3D, 2D and 1D return in a new form each time:
  - globe → map → line;
  - line → pencil drawing → painted world ("Loading");
  - the sheet flattening the globe into a drawing;
  - the witnesses becoming paper, then the world repainting outward from its lights.
- **Memory:** something can lose its body without losing what it left behind. Paper keeps the ochre marks of the lights, and the world repaints from them: "death is only a return".
- **Not used:**
  - **KV4b:** its local edit leaves a seam in B's hair.
  - **KV5a:** it has a smear where B's hands were.

  The pre-contact frame is KV5 with the flame filled in and its warm cast removed, so her hand is unlit until the fire.

Built: `paint/kvfilm.py`, the whole film, 0:00–3:33.4. Timings come from the measured section map (STORYBOARD_V3.md) and the beat map.

## The space layer (`paint/space.py`, my own shots)

These shots are my own 3D particle shots, not the KVs. The Earth is built from 34k real cities (geonamescache) and a real land mask. They use the same colour script as the KVs: warm is human, and stars, lattice and sheet are cold. Every space shot is rendered over the painted space of BG0a. Dimension here is literal geometry.
