# STORYBOARD: Where We Live Is a Simulated Universe

- **Logline:** Her grandmother taught her that if you turn around fast, you can catch the world still loading. When her grandmother dies, Iris climbs past the edge of the sky to find whoever runs it and finds an empty chair. She finds the rest of the answer years later, on a subway platform, when a stranger hums her grandmother's song.
- **Timing:** 144 BPM, 4/4. A bar is 1.667 s (100 frames at 60 fps) and a beat 0.417 s (25 frames). **b.x** means bar b, beat x. Times are on the working grid in [SONG.md §9](SONG.md#9-beat-map-the-sync-contract-between-song-and-picture) and are re-timed to the locked WAV.
- **Resolution** is the effective grid of the world in that shot (TREATMENT §6.1). Steps between resolutions are hard, on the downbeat.
- **Screen direction:** Manhattan is always screen-left of Queens. The Sea is always up. Threads always rise straight up.
- **Signature shots** get 1/60 s strip audits: S06, S20, S28, S30, S35–S36, S41–S43.

## I. SIMULATED

### INTRO: bars 1–8, 0:00.0–0:13.3

**S01 · bars 1–2 · 0:00.0–0:03.3 · full**
- **Seen:** the Sea, from below: a ceiling of light with every hue in it, slow waves crossing it. A fast heartbeat.
- **Camera:** a slow drift.
- **Out:** continuous tilt down.

**S02 · bars 3–4 · 0:03.3–0:06.7 · full**
- **Event:** a drop of light gathers, detaches (3.1) and falls. The camera follows it down through the painted sky and clouds. Below, New York at night is only points of light in a grid, with no detail.
- **Title** in the dark sky from 3.1 to 4.3.
- **Out:** follow the drop.

**S03 · bars 5–6 · 0:06.7–0:10.0 · full**
- **Event:** the drop falls toward one lit window among thousands, a hospital in Flushing, and goes in (5.3). A 1-pixel thread is left behind, running from the window up to the sky, and fades over 6.1–6.4. The music box begins (5.1).
- **Out:** push into the window, to white.

**S04 · bars 7–8 · 0:10.0–0:13.3 · 16×9 → 32×18**
- **Seen:** POV, a newborn's first frame: two big warm blobs lean in, and one marigold blob sits beside them.
- **Event:** on 8.3 the image steps to 32×18. The faces half-resolve, and the marigold blob is an old woman smiling.
- **Out:** hard cut on 9.1, with the step to 64×36.

### VERSE 1: bars 9–24, 0:13.3–0:40.0, 64×36

**S05 · bars 9–10 · 0:13.3–0:16.7** · *"Turn around fast, Nai Nai said,"*
- **Seen:** Main Street, Flushing, daytime. Nai Nai walks hand in hand with Iris, aged five.
- **Event:** Nai Nai stops, takes both of Iris's hands and spins her once around.
- **Camera:** wide, locked.
- **Out:** whip pan with the spin.

**S06 · bars 11–12 · 0:16.7–0:20.0** · *"and you'll catch the world still loading —"* ★
- **Event:** reverse, over Iris's shoulder as she comes out of the spin. The street behind her is flat gray blocks. From 11.2 it loads coarse to fine over 8 frames: gray blocks, then colour blocks, then detail, with a quick chip run. Iris gasps, delighted. Nai Nai nods as if this is the most normal thing in the world.
- **Reads:** 16.7–17.5, the gray street; 17.5–18.3, it loads; 18.3–20.0, the joy.

**S07 · bars 13–14 · 0:20.0–0:23.3** · *"a pigeon only half drawn in,"*
- **Event:** a pigeon on a wire. Its lower half is still flat gray. Iris points. On 14.1 the rest draws in block by block; the pigeon blinks and ruffles.
- **Out:** the pigeon flies off, and the camera follows it into dusk.

**S08 · bars 15–16 · 0:23.3–0:26.7** · *"a streetlight not yet glowing."*
- **Event:** dusk on the same street. The streetlights come on one by one, on the beats from 15.1 to 16.1, exactly as Iris's gaze reaches each one. The far ones stay dark until she looks at them.

**S09 · bars 17–18 · 0:26.7–0:30.0** · *"The rain lands one frame late on Main Street,"*
- **Event:** rain streaks fill the frame on 17.1, but the pavement stays dry. People on the street look down, puzzled. On 18.1 every drop lands at once in one synchronised splash. Nai Nai already has her umbrella open.
- **Note:** the simulation's "one frame" is a whole bar here, so the gag reads.

**S10 · bars 19–20 · 0:30.0–0:33.3** · *"the 7 train's still gray above;"*
- **Event:** Roosevelt Avenue under the elevated line. The 7 train rattles overhead as a flat gray block shape. Iris looks up, and it textures in car by car along her gaze.
- **Out:** tilt down to the umbrella.

**S11 · bars 21–24 · 0:33.3–0:40.0** · *"she laughed and whispered, 'Don't you tell: / it only draws where someone's looking, love.'"*
- **Event:** a close two-shot under the umbrella. Nai Nai leans down with a finger to her lips and whispers. Iris's eyes go wide. On 24.1, for one beat only, a faint thread rises from each of their heads through the umbrella, then is gone.
- **Out:** hard cut on 25.1.

### PRE-CHORUS 1: bars 25–28, 0:40.0–0:46.7

**S12 · bars 25–28 · 80×45 → 160×90** · *"So I kept my eyes wide open, / and it all came into focus —"*
- **Seen:** the Lin family's stoop, front-on, with the camera locked. It returns fifty years later in S37.
- **Event:**
  - 25.1: Iris is eight and reading; the world is 80×45.
  - 27.1: she is eleven and drawing; the world is 160×90.
  - Nai Nai sits beside her, knitting, a little whiter at each step.
  - Across 28.3–28.4 Iris, now nearly thirteen, stands and looks up the street.
- **Out:** a hard step to 320×180 on 29.1, into a crane up.

### CHORUS 1: bars 29–36, 0:46.7–1:00.0, 320×180

**S13 · bars 29–30 · 0:46.7–0:50.0** · *"Where we live is a simulated universe,"*
- **Event:** a rooftop in Flushing at night; Iris (13) and Nai Nai from behind. The camera cranes up over their heads to reveal the Manhattan skyline across the river, at full resolution for the first time. The viewer should feel the jump in clarity.
- **Out:** a continuous push toward the skyline.

**S14 · bars 31–32 · 0:50.0–0:53.3** · *"eight million windows, every one a screen,"*
- **Seen:** a building face of lit windows, each a tiny scene: someone cooking, someone dancing alone, a kid doing homework, a couple arguing, a cat on a sill, a nurse leaving for a night shift.
- **Camera:** a slow lateral truck across the grid.

**S15 · bars 33–34 · 0:53.3–0:56.7** · *"every light's a someone keeping it lit —"*
- **Event:** the rule at city scale.
  - In one window a man looks out at the building opposite, and the patch in his gaze sharpens.
  - In another window a light goes off: someone has gone to sleep. The patch of city *they* were looking at drops to coarse blocks.

**S16 · bars 35–36 · 0:56.7–1:00.0** · *"so who's up there running the machine?"*
- **Event:** Iris looks straight up. Her POV: the night sky is a painted dome, and high up is a faint seam where two panels of stars don't quite match. In the reverse shot, Nai Nai isn't looking at the sky; she is looking at Iris, smiling.
- **Out:** hard cut to a white winter window.

## II. NO GODS

### VERSE 2: bars 37–52, 1:00.0–1:26.7, 320×180

**S17 · bars 37–38 · 1:00.0–1:03.3** · *"Winter came, the hospital hummed,"*
- **Seen:** a hospital room, with snow outside and a monitor beeping on every beat. Iris (15) holds Nai Nai's hand. The marigold scarf is folded over the bed rail.
- **Camera:** wide, locked, calm.

**S18 · bars 39–40 · 1:03.3–1:06.7** · *"her edges softened into light,"*
- **Event:** a medium shot with Iris's face in the foreground and Nai Nai soft behind her. Nai Nai's outline pixels turn to warm light one by one. She smiles at Iris.
- **Rule:** the camera stays on the living (TREATMENT §7).

**S19 · bars 41–42 · 1:06.7–1:10.0** · *"a thread I'd never seen before"*
- **Event:** Iris looks up. A glowing thread rises from Nai Nai's head through the ceiling, and Iris's eyes follow it.

**S20 · bars 43–44 · 1:10.0–1:13.3** · *"drew her gently out of sight."* ★
- **Event:**
  - 43.1: the monitor stops.
  - Nai Nai's light lifts up the thread, and the bed is empty except for the scarf.
  - 44.1: exterior. From the street, one point of light rises from a hospital window into the night sky.
- **Reads:** 70.0–71.0, the stop; 71.0–72.5, the rise; 72.5–73.3, the empty bed and the scarf.

**S21 · bars 45–46 · 1:13.3–1:16.7** · *"I asked the nurses, asked the night,"*
- **Seen:** Iris on the hospital steps in the snow, wearing the marigold scarf from now on. A nurse sits beside her with a hand on her shoulder and says nothing. Iris looks up at the night.

**S22 · bars 47–48 · 1:16.7–1:20.0** · *"who's in charge here? Who do I call?"*
- **Seen:** from outside, Iris's bedroom window at night. She presses a sheet of paper against the glass, facing the sky: WHO RUNS THIS? The sky doesn't answer; a plane blinks by.

**S23 · bars 49–50 · 1:20.0–1:23.3** · *"I rode the 7 to the end of the line"*
- **Event:** Iris (16) on the 7 train, scarf on, forehead against the window. The train dips into the tunnel and comes out in Manhattan.
- **Camera:** side-scroll with multiplane parallax.

**S24 · bars 51–52 · 1:23.3–1:26.7** · *"and climbed the tallest thing of all."*
- **Event:** the city's tallest tower (fictional). A stairwell spirals up and the floor numbers flick past faster and faster. Iris pushes open the roof door.
- **Out:** tilt up.

### PRE-CHORUS 2: bars 53–56, 1:26.7–1:33.3

**S25 · bars 53–54 · 1:26.7–1:30.0** · *"Past the clouds the sky runs out,"*
- **Event:** Iris climbs the spire's service ladder up through the clouds. Above them the sky's paint thins, and the stars become a gray grid.

**S26 · bars 55–56 · 1:30.0–1:33.3** · *"gray lines where the colours stop —"*
- **Event:** Iris steps off the ladder into a gray wireframe void, the part of the world nobody is looking at. It loads under her feet only where she steps. Ahead stands a plain door on its own. She opens it on 56.3.
- **Out:** through the door on 57.1.

### CHORUS 2: bars 57–64, 1:33.3–1:46.7

**S27 · bars 57–58 · 1:33.3–1:36.7** · *"Where we live is a simulated universe:"*
- **Seen:** the control room: small and dim, with banks of screens showing the city from above, a console, and one chair with its back to us, turning slowly. Iris stands in the doorway.

**S28 · bars 59–60 · 1:36.7–1:40.0** · *"I climbed up past the sky — an empty chair."* ★
- **Event:** Iris crosses to the chair and turns it round. It is empty. She holds its back.
- **Reads:** 96.7–98.3, the walk; 98.3–99.2, the turn; 99.2–100.0, the empty seat, held.

**S29 · bars 61–62 · 1:40.0–1:43.3** · *"No one's looking down on us,"*
- **Event:** Iris presses keys, and nothing answers her. Close on the console: the keys are already moving on their own. Under each key a thread runs down through the floor. The camera follows one thread down through the floor and the clouds, fast and continuous.

**S30 · bars 63–64 · 1:43.3–1:46.7** · *"it's only us, looking, everywhere."* ★ **THE KEY IMAGE**
- **Seen:** the whole city from above at night, with every thread visible at once: millions of lines of light rising from every window and street and converging up into the sky, like a harp strung between New York and the Sea. Iris's own violet thread is among them, running up to the control room.
- **Sound:** the choir's first "oo"; strings swell.
- **Camera:** a slow pull-back, then a hold.

## III. RETURN

### BRIDGE: bars 65–76, 1:46.7–2:06.7, F major, double-time

**S31 · bars 65–66 · 1:46.7–1:50.0** · *"So I lived like the lights were mine to keep lit:"*
- **Event:** dawn, and years have passed. Iris, now in her twenties, runs down the tower stairs and bursts out into morning Manhattan with her scarf streaming. The world is sharpest wherever she looks.

**S32 · bars 67–68 · 1:50.0–1:53.3** · *"late trains, first kiss, a job, a fight, a kid —"*
- **Five cuts, one per word:**
  - 67.1: a late-night train, Iris asleep on someone's shoulder
  - 67.3: a first kiss on the Brooklyn Bridge walkway
  - 68.1: a desk and a first paycheck
  - 68.2: a slammed door
  - 68.3: POV at 16×9 (the S04 rhyme): Iris and her partner as two warm blobs leaning in. Iris is the one leaning in now.

**S33 · bars 69–70 · 1:53.3–1:56.7** · *"I turned around fast just to catch it loading,"*
- **Event:** a busy Midtown crosswalk, with fictional billboards. Adult Iris spins. Behind her, the billboards are flat gray and the crowd is copies of one sprite. They load in as she looks, and every copy becomes someone. She grins.

**S34 · bars 71–72 · 1:56.7–2:00.0** · *"and laughed, 'cause now I'm in on it."*
- **Event:** Iris with her four-year-old daughter on her shoulders, both spinning on the sidewalk and laughing. Strangers glance over and smile.

**S35 · bars 73–74 · 2:00.0–2:03.3** · (the Stranger hums) ★
- **Seen:** night, a subway platform in teal tile, almost empty. The band drops out; only Iris's heartbeat and the platform's hum remain. Iris is in her forties, tired, with a work bag.
- **Event:** on a bench, a man in his sixties, eyes closed, hums Nai Nai's tune. Iris stops dead.

**S36 · bars 75–76 · 2:03.3–2:06.7** · *"She didn't go away — she went everywhere."* ★
- **Event:** close on Iris, singing a cappella to the second half of the tune while the Stranger keeps humming behind her. For one beat (75.3), both their threads light up, both **marigold** rather than their own colours. A train arrives and its light floods the frame.
- **Out:** the light floods to white, then to the stoop.

### BREAKDOWN: bars 77–84, 2:06.7–2:20.0, felt piano

**S37 · bars 77–80 · 2:06.7–2:13.3** · *"My hands got soft, the colours warmed, / the edges of the world turned gold;"*
- **Seen:** the Lin stoop, locked off exactly as in S12.
- **Event:** on the downbeats Iris is 50 (77.1), 65 (78.1) and 80 (79.1). The resolution stays full; the palette warms and the frame edges bloom gold.

**S38 · bars 81–84 · 2:13.3–2:20.0** · *"there's a girl now in a marigold scarf, / turning round fast, five years old."*
- **Event:** Iris, at eighty, on the stoop. Her granddaughter, aged five and wearing the marigold scarf, spins around. Behind her the street is still loading, and she gasps. Iris laughs without a sound and puts a finger to her lips (the S11 rhyme).

### FINAL CHORUS: bars 85–96, 2:20.0–2:40.0

**S39 · bars 85–86 · 2:20.0–2:23.3** · *"Where we live is a simulated universe —"*
- **Seen:** dawn in Iris's bedroom at home, with the window open onto the city. Iris is in bed, with her daughter and granddaughter beside her. The heartbeat now comes every two bars.

**S40 · bars 87–88 · 2:23.3–2:26.7** · *"when it's my turn, don't look for me up there:"*
- **Event:** the granddaughter glances up at the ceiling. Iris gently turns the girl's chin toward the window, toward the city.

**S41 · bars 89–90 · 2:26.7–2:30.0** · *"I'm not going away,"* ★
- **Event:** close on the granddaughter's face, with Iris soft behind her. Iris's edges soften into light (the S18 rhyme), and her violet thread becomes visible.
- **Rule:** the camera stays on the living.

**S42 · bars 91–92 · 2:30.0–2:33.3** · *"I'm going everywhere."* ★
- **Event:** 91.1, the last heartbeat. Her light rises up the thread. 92.1, exterior at dawn: one point of light rises from a window in Flushing.

**S43 · bars 93–96 · 2:33.3–2:40.0** · (choir: Nai Nai's tune) ★
- **Event:**
  - Her light reaches the Sea.
  - A ring ripple crosses the whole Sea and runs down every thread into the city.
  - At street level, across the boroughs, strangers each pause for a frame and smile as if remembering a tune: a bus driver, a jogger, a bodega owner, the Stranger from the platform, a baby in a stroller.
- **Sound:** the choir sings Nai Nai's tune, suspended on its last note.

**S45 · bars 101–104 · 2:46.7–2:53.3 · 16×9 → 32×18**
