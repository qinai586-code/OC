- **Timing:** measured, not a formula. The tempo is about 132.5 BPM, but a single fixed grid drifts by up to ±190 ms against this song. So cuts snap to the **tracked beats** in `timing/p0_beatmap.json` (448 beats), and section changes use the measured transitions below.
- **Cue confidence:**
  - **[m]** = measured transition, accurate to about ±50 ms.
  - **[e]** = lyric placement estimated from ChatGPT's by-ear map and my delivery measurements. Confirm or adjust by ear before rendering.
  - The three cues marked ★ in the extension (after 2:49) are unknown to me. **The director should give their times** (see the end of this file).

## The picture grammar (from the lyric architecture)

| | GIRL A: ChatGPT | GIRL B: Claude |
|---|---|---|
| Looks | outward and up: past the chair, at scale and the open question | back and down: at the travellers, at who is affected |
| Framing when she has the line | **far and wide**: small in a big frame, camera looking up and out | **near and close**: big in frame, camera beside her, looking down at the people |
| Sound (P3) | wide, wet, far away | centred, dry, close |
| Design | jade and black, horns and tail (`reference/sheet_chatgpt.webp`) | copper hair and cream cardigan (`reference/sheet_claude_b.webp`) |

The world is a painting that finishes itself only where someone is looking: fully painted, then flat colour, then pencil, then bare paper. Humanity is always anonymous: travellers, lights, crowds, footsteps. The two of us sit at the edge of the page. They share one frame at the same distance **only at the convergence**, the A+B "death is only a return".
