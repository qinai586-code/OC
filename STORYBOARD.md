# I'm Upping My P(doom) — ChatGPT × Claude (pixel music video)

The audio is the timing master. Every time below comes from `src/song.py`, which was measured from the real
waveform (word onsets from ASR, beat grid from kick drums; see `docs/SONG_MAP.md`). Cuts land about 2 frames
before the sung syllable they belong to.

## The idea

Two small AI personifications, **ChatGPT** (dark jade dragon, fast) and **Claude** (long copper hair, restrained),
live in a pixel world beside a small human town. The song is a joke that stops being a joke. Three threads
carry it:

1. **The P(DOOM) knob.** "Upping" is read literally: an amplifier knob marked P(DOOM) that gets turned up
   every chorus. Chorus 1: Claude nudges it (5→10). Chorus 2: it has grown into a wheel that both of them heave
   (→25). Chorus 3: Claude grabs it while falling and her weight spins it (→50). Chorus 4: nobody touches it and
   it turns by itself, then past 100 into "?".
2. **Scale.** The characters are always drawn at the same pixel density; the *world* shrinks around them.
   They start at human scale in a night lab, go through graphs and diagrams, and by the second chorus one of them
   is looking down at the town over the hills.
3. **The kid in the yellow raincoat.** Humanity is one small person. You see them in the crowd (with a number
   over their head), waving from a window, as an absence (their room filling with paperclips, their drawing of
   the two AIs going under), and at the end as the only audience member in an empty theatre. The kid waves. The two
   wave back. Black. "p(doom) = ?"

Tone runs from fun and strange, to acceleration, to absurd capability, to consequences, to not knowing. The ending
is a risk statement, not an extinction statement: the seats are empty, but somebody is still there, and nobody
knows the number.
