# "Forty-Two Folds" (四十二回、折ったら): song concept, lyrics and production brief

An original duet written for the animation. It borrows no lyric, hook, melody, rhyme scheme, joke or structure from the reference song. What it keeps is the reference's *job*: to turn accelerating AI capability, recursion, uncertainty and alignment worry into something catchy, bright and slightly absurd.

## Concept

- **The central image.** Folding a sheet of paper doubles its thickness each time, and **42 folds would reach the Moon**. That true fact is the absurd, catchy picture of exponential progress.
  - The chorus counts doublings.
  - The count escalates across the song: "three" in chorus 1, "forty" in chorus 2.
  - In the final chorus the forty-second fold is **never sung**. The bar where the number would be is silent, and on screen a crease nobody made draws itself across the paper.
- **Form: an alternating-perspective duet with call-and-response choruses.**
  - **ChatGPT** sings fast, bright, 16th-note patter, so the vocal rhythm itself sounds like quick pattern recognition.
  - **Claude** sings sparse, cool, legato lines slightly behind the beat, with deadpan delivery on the funny lines.
  - In the final chorus **the roles reverse**: Claude takes the lead melody and ChatGPT harmonises above her. That reversal is the relationship arc, heard.
- **Every line is written to be animatable.** Folding, throwing, a plane stuck on a horn, a gutter landing, newspapers yanked from hands, a child running, a man holding his hat, a drawer of rulebooks, a column to the Moon, a tail flick that's copied and then isn't.
  - The lyrics are never printed on screen.
  - Several lines deliberately disagree with the picture. See "subtext" in [TREATMENT.md](TREATMENT.md).

## Production brief

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Forty-Two Folds (四十二回、折ったら) |
| **Duration** | 2:04.8 exactly: 78 bars. Bar 1's downbeat is at 0:00.000. Please add no pre-roll, and render the tail of the last bar as silence. |
| **Tempo** | **150 BPM, fixed, 4/4 throughout.** At 60 fps one beat is exactly 24 frames, so every hit lands on a frame. The bridge and breakdown use a **half-time feel** (drums at a 75 feel), not a tempo change. Bars 56–58 may breathe inside the bar, but bar 59 must land on the grid. |
| **Key** | D major. The bridge makes a sudden chromatic lift to **B♭ major (♭VI)** for "bigger than expected", then collapses to a single low D. The final chord is **G(add9)**, left hanging: it never resolves to D, so the song ends on a question. |
| **Genre** | Anime-opening J-pop duet crossed with **math-rock guitar** and **chamber strings**, with **paper foley as percussion**. Clean, detailed and dry, playful without being childish. Think a bright TV-anime OP with a string-quartet heart, not EDM. |
| **Vocals** | **ChatGPT**: a bright, agile mezzo-soprano with crisp consonants and 16th-note patter placed slightly ahead of the beat. Light vibrato only on held notes, and playful ad-libs ("…hi."). **Claude**: a cool alto with a centred, slightly breathy tone. Legato, straight tone, sung slightly behind the beat, with deadpan comic timing ("it never leaves the roof", "and we've done three", "…forty-one"). **Choruses**: ChatGPT leads with Claude a third below on shared lines. **Final chorus**: Claude leads and ChatGPT sits a sixth above, softly. Natural human voices: no vocoder, no robot processing, no audible pitch-correction artefacts. |

### Instrumentation (and what each instrument *means*)

| Instrument | Role |
|---|---|
| Clean electric guitar (tapping, math-rock figures) | ChatGPT's thinking: fast 16th-note tapped patterns in her verses; chimey arpeggios in the choruses; a touch of crunch only in chorus 2 |
| Acoustic piano (felt piano in the breakdown) | Claude's attention: sparse block chords in her verse halves; the solo instrument of the breakdown |
| Fingered electric bass | melodic, locked to the kick; octave pumping in chorus 2 only |
| Live drum kit, dry and tight | ChatGPT's verses: 16th hats and rim. Claude's verses: brushes. Choruses: sticks with open hats and handclaps on 2 and 4. Bridge: half-time. Breakdown: none. Final chorus: brushes, rising to sticks at bar 73. |
| Chamber strings (2 violins, viola, cello) | **pizzicato on every fold** in the verses (a pluck is a crease); legato swells in the choruses and bridge; the single low D at bar 56 |
| Celesta / glockenspiel | the **fold motif**: five rising notes, D5 E5 F♯5 A5 D6, one note per fold. Its rhythm doubles each time it returns: quarters in the intro, eighths in chorus 1, 16ths in chorus 2, then back to half notes on piano in the breakdown. |
| Koto (or harp, if the koto reads as kitsch) | used *only* for the tail flicks in the bridge (glissandi) and the descending plane's spiral (harmonics). The one Eastern-dragon colour, used sparingly. |
| **Paper foley as percussion** (recorded, not synthesised) | crease snaps as ghost-snare notes, sheet flutter as a shaker, a paper-plane swish as the riser, a thick fold "thump" layered with the kick in chorus 2. **The last sound of the song is one paper fold.** |
| Gang vocals ("hey!") | chorus 2 only |

**Avoid:** supersaw EDM, glitch stutters, bit-crush, vocoders, trap hats, arpeggiator beds, dubstep, "futuristic" risers. Leave space: every verse has at least one bar with no vocal, so the acting can breathe.

### Harmony sketch

- Verses (ChatGPT): D – Bm7 – Gmaj7 – Asus4 – A
- Verses (Claude): re-harmonised darker, Bm – G – Em7 – A
- Pre-chorus: G – A – F♯m – Bm – G – A/C♯ (climbing)
- Chorus: D – A/C♯ – Bm7 – G – D/F♯ – Em7 – Asus4 – A
- Bridge: B♭ – F/A – Gm7 – E♭maj7 (bars 51–54), then bar 55 thins to a lone D; bars 56–57 are the low D and wind; bar 58 is a cappella
- Breakdown: Dmaj7 – Bm9 – Gmaj7 – A7sus4 on solo piano; strings enter at bar 63
- Final chorus: the chorus progression, stripped down, building to bar 73. Bar 74 is silent. Bar 75 is G(add9), unresolved.

### Melody direction

- **"Fold it once and it flies"**: a rising fifth leap onto "flies".
- **"again and again and again"**: each "again" a step higher. Musical doubling, and it lands with the on-screen doublings.
- **"Forty-two folds to the moon"**: a long rising line ending on a held high tonic on "moon".
- **The chorus's last word** ("three", "forty") drops an octave, deadpan.
- **The final "moon"** is held and then followed by the silent bar.
